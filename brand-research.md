## The food (verbatim from the Penang Foodie feature + posts)
Hudson's is a real European-style deli. Menu narrative from the reel:
- **House-roasted meats** — pastrami / roast beef (~150g of beef per sandwich), **lamb roasted in-house**.
- **Imported cured meats** — **Sopressa** & **Milano** salami, **Pancetta**.
- **Cheeses** — **Gorgonzola** (from Italy), and others.
- **Signature sandwiches** — **The Reuben** (the hero), **Smoked Beef Panini**, pastrami sandwiches, all with **homemade spicy sauce**.
- **Soup** — **Goulash Soup** (the Austrian touch).
- **Coffee** — served (iced coffee seen in photos).
- Everything "**Made with [h] every day**".

## Brand visual identity (observed)
- **Logo:** a lowercase **"h"** inside a **circular badge/seal**. Appears on signage, merch, aprons.
- **Aesthetic:** **vintage Americana deli / retro poster** — see `poster-reuben.jpg`: cream/parchment background, deep brick red, navy ink, distressed letterpress display type, starbursts, "GOOD FOOD GOOD MOOD" ribbon, smiley seal.
- **Storefront:** navy-blue shopfront awning with white "h HUDSON'S DELI" sign, wood + plants, jars of preserves on shelves, wall of knives, deli slicer, panini grill.
- **Merch:** branded tote bags & t-shirts carrying the Reuben poster art (strong community following).

### Suggested palette (derived from the poster + storefront)
- Parchment / cream `#F4EAD5` (backgrounds)
- Deli brick red `#B5321E` / deeper `#8E2417` (primary accent, signage)
- Navy ink `#1C2B4A` (text, shopfront)
- Mustard / gold `#E0A22B` (secondary accent, "7 years" laurel gold)
- Charcoal near-black `#211C18` (deep text)
- Pastrami pink `#C46A6A` / roast-crust brown `#5A3620` (food-tone supporting hues)

### Type direction
- Display: a **retro condensed slab / vintage grotesque** with letterpress character (deli signage feel).
- Body: a clean humanist sans for readability.
- Optional accent: a script/handwritten for "Good Mood"-style flourishes.
